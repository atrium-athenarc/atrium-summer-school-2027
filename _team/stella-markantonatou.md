Dr. Stella Markantonatou holds an MA in Chemical Engineering (NTUA 1983), BA in Linguistics (NKUA 1988) and a PhD in Linguistics (University of Essex 1992). She is an Emeritus Research Director at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing/ATHENA RC and a Principal Researcher in the AI Unit Archimedes/ATHENA RC. Her expertise lies in NLP for under-resourced language varieties with a focus on Modern Greek dialects and idiomatic speech, in language resource development and in knowledge representation.
