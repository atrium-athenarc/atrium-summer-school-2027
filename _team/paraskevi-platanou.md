Paraskevi Platanou is a PhD candidate at the Department of Informatics of the Athens University of Economics and Business and  a fellow at Archimedes Unit, Athena RC, working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Digital Humanities, and written cultural heritage. Her research focuses on OCR/HTR, document image analysis, and pattern recognition for the study of ancient Greek papyri, manuscripts and inscriptions, with particular interests in low-resource learning, explainable AI, and computational palaeography. She holds degrees in Classical Philology and English Language and Literature, an MSc in Digital Methods for the Humanities, and an MA in Philology with a specialization in Papyrology and Classical Literature.
