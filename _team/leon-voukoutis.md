Leon Voukoutis has been a scientific associate at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) of the Athena Research Centre (Athena RC) since April 2020. His work focuses on machine learning, NLP and language technologies, with particular emphasis on the development and evaluation of language resources, infrastructures and LLMs. He has contributed to the development of open Greek LLMs, including Meltemi, the first open Large Language Model for Greek, and Krikri.
