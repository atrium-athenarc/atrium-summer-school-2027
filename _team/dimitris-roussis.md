Dimitris Roussis is a Scientific Associate and Data Scientist at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) of Athena Research Center, and co-founder of Layer42 Labs. He holds an MSc in Data Science and Information Technologies from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and an Integrated MEng in Civil Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens. His research focuses mainly on LLM training, including multilingual corpus creation, data processing, synthetic data generation, post-training, inference, and evaluation. He has been responsible for the data pipelines behind Meltemi and Krikri, the first open Greek LLMs, and has contributed to several other projects such as Pharos, LLMs4EU, SciLake, ELRC3. Additionally, he has worked on machine translation, parallel corpus construction, and language resource development. His work has been published in venues including EMNLP, EAMT, WMT, and LREC.
