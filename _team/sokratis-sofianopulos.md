Sokratis Sofianopoulos received his Ph.D. from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National Technical University of Athens in 2010, in the field of Natural Language Processing. Since 2005 he has been a Research Associate at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing of Athena Research Center, working on machine translation, multilingual NLP and large language models. He has participated in more than twenty national and European research projects and is part of the team behind Meltemi and Llama-Krikri, the first open generative large language models for Greek, and designs applied AI systems for public organisations and companies. He also teaches Programming for Language Technology and Machine Translation in the M.Sc. in Language Technology at the University of Athens. Since 2017 he has been an adjunct lecturer at HAEC, teaching courses in machine learning, neural networks, NLP and others.
