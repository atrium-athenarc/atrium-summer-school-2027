Stergios Chatzikyriakidis is Professor of Computational Linguistics at the Department of Philology, University of Crete, where he directs the Computational Linguistics and Language Technology Lab. He is also Head of Research at MuVeS AI and founder of SimasiaAI. His work lies at the intersection of NLP, formal semantics and type theory, the reasoning and evaluation of large language models, Computatonal Dialectology and low-resource varieties. He holds an MSc and a PhD from King's College London.
