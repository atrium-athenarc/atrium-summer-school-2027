Stavros Bompolas is a researcher at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) of the Athena Research Center. He holds a PhD in Linguistics, specializing in Computational Dialectology, and previously conducted postdoctoral research at the Archimedes Unit of Athena RC. His research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, dialectology, historical linguistics, dialectometry, and phylogenetics, with a particular focus on Modern Greek dialects. His current work focuses on the development of linguistic resources and infrastructures for dialectal varieties, as well as the development and evaluation of AI-based language technologies for low-resource language varieties. He has participated in several national and international research projects on Modern Greek dialects, language resources, and language technology, and his research has been published in international journals, edited volumes, and conference proceedings.
