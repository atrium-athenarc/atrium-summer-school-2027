Prokopis Prokopidis is a researcher and computational linguist at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) / Athena RC, where he has worked since 1998. He holds a degree in English Language and Literature from the University of Athens and an MA in Computational Linguistics from the University of Essex. He has participated in Greek and European projects focused on natural language processing, information extraction, and language resource development. He has contributed to the creation of annotated corpora, such as the Greek Dependency Treebank, as well as tools for automatic text annotation. His recent research includes named-entity recognition for archaeological texts, anonymization for court decisions, and the development of evaluation resources for Greek large language models. He has published in conferences, journals, and edited volumes.
