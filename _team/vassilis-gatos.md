Basilis G. Gatos received his Electrical Engineering Diploma and Ph.D. degree in Optical Character Recognition Techniques from the Democritus University of Thrace, Greece. He currently serves as a Researcher at the Institute of Informatics and Telecommunications of NCSR "Demokritos" in Athens, Greece, and is a co-founder of the spin-off companies AI4Doc and Astros Intelligence. His professional career spans leadership roles in R&D at Lambrakis Press Archives, Computer Logic S.A., and BSI S.A. With research interests focused on Image Processing, Document Image Analysis, OCR, and Pattern Recognition, Dr. Gatos has authored over 200 publications in international journals and conference proceedings and participated in numerous EU-funded research programs. A member of the Technical Chamber of Greece, he regularly serves on program committees for major international conferences (such as ICDAR, ICFHR, and DAS) and co-organized ICFHR 2014, DAS 2016, and ICDAR 2024 in Greece.
