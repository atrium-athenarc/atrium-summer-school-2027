Vivian Stamou is an Associate Researcher at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) at Athena Research Center. She previously served as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Archimedes Unit of Athena Research Center. She holds a PhD in Computational Psycholinguistics from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and an MSc in Computational Linguistics from the University of Stuttgart. Her research interests include modeling low-resource languages, developing resources and models for offensive language detection and investigating the role of bias in language technologies.
