Katerina Gkirtzou is a Scientific Associate at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) at Athena R.C. She holds a PhD in Applied Mathematics from École Centrale Paris, where she investigated sparsity regularization and graph-based representation in medical imaging. She has participated in designing and building major European infrastructures for language resources — including CLARIN:EL, the European Language Grid and Language Data Space. Her current research focuses on grounding language technology in structured knowledge, with particular emphasis on modelling lexical and linguistic resources as Linked Data (OntoLex/LLOD), knowledge graphs and neuro-symbolic methods.
