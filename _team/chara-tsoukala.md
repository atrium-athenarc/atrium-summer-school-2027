Chara Tsoukala is a Research Associate at the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) at Athena R.C. She holds an MSc in Human-Machine Communication from the University of Groningen and a PhD from Radboud University, where she investigated bilingual sentence production and code-switching through cognitive computational modeling. Her current research focuses on multilingual and low-resource language technologies, with particular emphasis on Automatic Speech Recognition for under-resourced languages, Natural Language Processing, and LLM-based agents and multi-agent systems.
