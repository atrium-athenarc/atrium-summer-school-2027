Giorgos Paraskevopoulos (Member, IEEE) received the Dipl. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering in 2016 and the Ph.D. degree in Machine Learning for Speech and Natural Language Processing in 2024, both from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA). His doctoral research focused on cognitive-inspired machine learning methods for dimensionality reduction and cross-domain adaptation in resource-constrained environments. He is currently an Associate Researcher (Researcher Grade C) with the Institute for Language and Speech Processing (ILSP) at the Athena Research Center, where he has been a scientific collaborator since 2020. His industry experience includes roles at Intracom Telecom (SDN/NFV cloud engineering), Behavioral Signals (emotion recognition and MLOps), and two research internships with Amazon: at Alexa AI (Lab126) focusing on multimodal speech recognition, and at AWS AI Labs focusing on multimodal video action recognition and summarization. Dr. Paraskevopoulos’s research focus is multimodal machine learning, with additional interests in large language model (LLM) development, contributing to the Greek LLM Meltemi and Llama-krikri. His current research interests focus on multimodal interpretability. He has authored numerous papers in peer-reviewed journals and international conferences, garnering over 100 citations (h-index 17). He serves as a reviewer for leading scientific venues and is a member of ISCA. Since 2024, he has also been a lead instructor and co-instructor for graduate courses in Speech Synthesis, Multimodal Dialogue Systems, and Deep Learning at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA).
