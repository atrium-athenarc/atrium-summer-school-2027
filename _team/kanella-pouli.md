Kanella Pouli holds a BSc in Linguistics from the University of Athens and an MSc in Language Technology from the University of Athens and the National Technical University of Athens. She has been a Scientific Associate at ILSP/Athena RC since 2012 and has contributed to the following European and national projects: QTLaunchPad, CEF-ELRC, FutureTDM, ELRC+L2, CRACKER, ISD, CLARIN:EL, Apollonis, ELE, LDS, LLMs4EU, and AltEdic4EU. Her work focuses on the evaluation, usability, and continuous improvement of research infrastructures for language resources and technologies. As a research infrastructure specialist, she contributes to user-centred design, testing, and quality assurance. She also develops technical documentation and training materials and supports users through workshops, seminars, and training sessions.
